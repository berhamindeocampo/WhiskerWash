Hitori "Bocchi" Gotoh from Kessoku Band cosplaying as a catgirl in local prefecture

By Beruhamin • May 27, 2026

What a great find!

In a surprising turn of events, Hitori Gotoh, also known as Bocchi-chan, the beloved guitarist from Kessoku Band, was spotted in a local prefecture dressed as a catgirl. Fans were thrilled to see Hitori embracing her inner feline, complete with adorable cat ears and a fluffy tail. The sighting has sparked a wave of excitement among fans, who are eager to see more of Hitori's playful side in future appearances. Whether this is a one-time cosplay or the start of a new trend for Hitori remains to be seen, but one thing is certain: the catgirl look suits her perfectly!
